# Pokédex tracking

People track Pokémon collection progress against Pokédexes for individual games and across games.

## Language

**Tracker**:
A user's independent checklist for a chosen Pokédex. A user can have multiple trackers for the same Pokédex and decides what checking an entry means.
_Avoid_: Pokédex, when referring to an individual user's checklist

**Regional Pokédex**:
A game's numbered list of Pokémon for a particular region, such as Paldea, Kitakami, or Blueberry.

**Combined Pokédex**:
A game's collection list containing its base Pokédex followed by entries added by each DLC, without repeating the same Pokémon variant. A regional variant remains distinct from other variants of its species.

**National Pokédex**:
The list of all Pokémon species across games, ordered by National Pokédex number.

**Form**:
A distinct variant of a Pokémon species, such as Hisuian Growlithe. Forms can share a species' Pokédex number.

**Regional variant**:
A form associated with a particular region, such as Galarian Meowth or Johtonian Wooper.

**White-Striped Basculin**:
The Basculin variant occupying Kitakami's numbered Basculin entry. It is distinct from the combined Scarlet and Violet list's ordinary Basculin entry, which covers both Red-Striped and Blue-Striped forms.

**Extra regional entries**:
Regional variants and their evolutions that supplement a regional Pokédex's numbered list. These entries can include distinct species such as Perrserker and Quagsire.

**Sync conflict**:
Pending local tracker changes alongside remote changes made since the last successful synchronization. Resolving a conflict requires the user's acknowledgement before either state is replaced.

**Box**:
A fixed arrangement of slots in a game's Pokémon storage. A tracker uses the relevant game's box dimensions to make comparison with that storage easier.

**Acquisition source**:
A way to obtain a Pokémon in a specified game, such as an encounter, gift, evolution, trade, or transfer.
_Avoid_: Data source, which refers to the reference used to establish game information
